## WhatsApp ban prevention (strict consent, opt-out, quiet hours, pacing)

Reduces preventable reasons WhatsApp bans a Baileys number. These are safeguards, not a guarantee — never label anything "anti-ban" or "safe". Branch `feat/whatsapp-ban-prevention` (separate from the D1 security work).

- **Three pure modules** (tests alongside): `src/lib/wa-optout.ts` (`isOptOut`, detects "baja/stop/ya no me escribas", never fires on "cancelar mi cita"), `src/lib/wa-pacing.ts` (quiet hours with midnight wrap, per-instance per-run cap of 3), `src/lib/wa-consent.ts` (`mayStartMessage`: SHADOW measures, ENFORCE blocks; opt-out blocks in both; a visit created before `messagingEnforcedAt` is grandfathered).
- **Consent sources** (`appointments.whatsappConsentAt/whatsappConsentSource`, `bookingChannel`): `WHATSAPP_BOOKING` (bot, only when the sender books for themselves — booking for another person is not consent), `WEB_CHECKBOX` (optional unchecked box on `/agendar`), `PANEL_STAFF` (staff checkbox in "Nueva cita"). Holding a phone number is never consent.
- **Reminder gate** (`src/lib/reminder-run.ts`, fed by `reminder-data.ts`): a consent-blocked visit (ENFORCE only) sends nothing and is never released, so it counts as confirmed; opt-out blocks in both modes; a night reminder is held for the morning when the visit still allows it; at most 3 sends per number per 15-min run, the rest deferred. The run response carries `blocked`, `shadowWouldBlock`, `deferred`. Default mode is SHADOW per tenant.
- **Opt-out**: `whatsapp/sync` runs `isOptOut` on inbound text, sets `whatsapp_chats.optedOutAt` and turns the bot off for that contact (`botPausedReason: "opted_out"`). A later message never re-enables it; only a new explicit consent does. Reminders and the agenda badge match the opt-out to the visit by `patientKeyOf` (`optedOutKeysOf` in `src/lib/whatsapp-permission.ts`; before 2026-11-20 the agenda compared raw digits and missed visits stored as `+52…`/`52…`).
- **Opt-out detector (QA 2026-11-20)**: it fires only on a request, never on a complaint about the past or on one item. "No me escribieron el link", "el doctor ya no me escribió", "No me manden la ubicación" and "No me envíe recordatorio" are not opt-outs; "no me escriban", "ya no me manden nada", "no me envíen más mensajes" are. Tests: `tests/wa-optout.test.ts`.
- **Opt-out in "Necesitan ayuda" (QA 2026-11-20)**: a detected opt-out also raises the help reason `opted_out` (registry `src/lib/help-reasons.ts`: medium priority, per chat, `pausesBot: false` so "Marcar atendido" keeps the bot off). The open chat shows a band ("Pidió no recibir mensajes…") with **Reactivar mensajes**: a person ticks "Confirmé con el paciente…" first (a checkbox in the band; the button stays disabled until it is ticked, no native dialog), then `POST /api/apps/whatsapp/chats/[id]/help` with `action: "reactivate"` clears `optedOutAt`, records `messagingReactivatedAt` and `messagingReactivatedById` (that record is the new explicit consent), turns the bot back on only when the opt-out paused it, and closes only the `opted_out` help request (`reactivateMessagingPatch` in `src/lib/help-actions.ts`). Turning the bot on with the switch never lifts an opt-out (`PATCH /api/apps/whatsapp/chats/[id]` answers 409 `whatsapp_chat_opted_out`). Schema: `prisma/sql/33-chat-messaging-reactivation` (two nullable columns), **applied to production on 2026-11-20** after a verified `pg_dump` backup (kept outside the repo) and an approval; tested before on a throwaway PostgreSQL 17 restored from that backup (diff before, after `up.sql`, after `down.sql`, after `up.sql` again), and `prisma migrate diff` of the real database against the schema was empty afterwards.
- **Settings** (`PATCH /api/tenants/reminders`, reminders card): quiet-hours window (`quietHoursStartMin/EndMin`, default 21:00–08:00) and `messagingPolicyMode`; switching to ENFORCE stamps `messagingEnforcedAt` (grandfathers existing visits).
- **No reminder inside the quiet hours (QA 2026-11-20)**: with the weekly hours (`Tenant.businessHours`), the reminder lead and the quiet window, every visit time maps to one reminder time (`reminderQuietConflict` in `src/lib/reminder-quiet-hours.ts`, tests `tests/reminder-quiet-hours.test.ts`). The reminders card shows "Con tu horario, una cita a las 09:00 recibiría su recordatorio a las 06:00…" and disables Save; `PATCH /api/tenants/reminders` answers 400 `reminder_in_quiet_hours`, only when the save touches the timing (so the "Mensajes al cliente" card, same route, is never blocked). `GET` returns `businessHours` for the card. On 2026-11-20 both businesses in the database (doctorgcenter, newy) already had a conflicting setup: their card opens with the error until the lead or the quiet hours change.
- **Night hold never passes the deadline (QA 2026-11-20)**: `shouldHoldForQuietHours` (`src/lib/wa-pacing.ts`) holds a night reminder only when, after the quiet window ends, at least 30 minutes remain before the confirmation deadline (or the visit when it asks no confirmation). Before, a reminder held from 22:00 went out at 08:00 saying "confirm before 08:00" and the visit was released at 08:15. The reminder job also sends with `urgent: true` (`reminder-data.ts`): the job already applied the quiet rule, and without it the messaging service held the same reminder again until the morning, after the deadline or after the visit. Regression: `tests/reminder-quiet-deadline.test.ts`.
- **Schema**: `prisma/sql/30-whatsapp-ban-prevention` (additive). **Applied to production** (confirmed read-only on 2026-11-20: every column exists, and `prisma migrate diff` of the real database against the schema of PR #130 was empty).

### Anti-ban messaging service (B0, B2, B3; merged in PR #130 on 2026-11-19)

- **One path for every outbound message.** `sendWhatsAppMessage` (`src/lib/whatsapp.ts`) calls `sendOutbound` (`src/lib/messaging/service.ts`) with the production gateway (`src/lib/messaging/gateway.ts`). The send call sites declare their purpose: `CUSTOMER_REPLY` (the owner answers a chat), `TRANSACTIONAL` (visit reminders, cancellations, confirmations) and `INTERNAL` (notices to the owner or an assistant's own number, `recipientIsTeam`). Reminders and cancellations use the idempotency keys `reminder:<visitId>` and `cancel:<visitId>`.
- **The decision** (`src/lib/messaging/policy.ts`, pure): kill switches (global, business, instance) and risk states stop every purpose; marketing is blocked; opt-out blocks transactional messages and customer replies in both modes; no consent is measured in SHADOW and blocks in ENFORCE; quiet hours defer a non-urgent visit message (held, not dropped).
- **Delivery rules** (`src/lib/messaging/delivery.ts`, pure): a SENT message is never resent; an UNKNOWN one (a timeout after the request left) is never resent blindly; a missing setup is retried later without using an attempt; a 4xx or auth failure closes the row. The text of a message is stored only until it is sent or closed. The outbox keeps only the last four digits of the recipient.
- **Circuit breaker**: an auth error or a logged-out session (401/403 on close) pauses the instance at once (`riskState = SENDING_PAUSED`, reason `possible_restriction` / `auth`). Repeated failures pause it after five in a row. Nothing resumes it by itself: `POST /api/apps/whatsapp/risk/resume` needs `confirmReviewed: true`.
- **Connection events**: Evolution's webhook now subscribes to `CONNECTION_UPDATE` (`evolution-adapter.ts`). `POST /api/internal/whatsapp/connection` (internal key) applies `src/lib/messaging/connection.ts`: an open event records the time and never clears a pause.
- **Kill switches**: the business switch is `Tenant.whatsappKillSwitch`, set with `PATCH /api/apps/whatsapp/risk` (`businessKillSwitch`). The instance switch is `connected_apps.killSwitch`. The global switch is the one row of `platform_flags` (`globalKillSwitch`), set by an operator in the database, never from the app.
- **Status card**: `src/components/admin/WhatsAppRiskCard.tsx` in the WhatsApp settings tab shows the state with an icon and a word, the reason when known, the business pause, and the resume action after a confirmation. It never shows a percentage and never calls a restriction a confirmed ban.
- **Adapter**: all calls to Evolution go through `src/lib/messaging/evolution-adapter.ts` (TLS validated, timeout 15 s, per-instance token). The insecure agents were removed (`bd1e48d`).
- **Schema**: `prisma/sql/31-whatsapp-outbox` (table `whatsapp_outbox`) and `prisma/sql/32-whatsapp-risk` (columns on `connected_apps` and `tenants`, table `platform_flags`). Both are additive and were verified up and down on the local demo database. **Applied to production** (confirmed read-only on 2026-11-20: `whatsapp_outbox`, `platform_flags`, `connected_apps.killSwitch/riskState` and `tenants.whatsappKillSwitch` exist; commit `9fb3c72` records 32 being applied).
- **Held messages**: a message held for quiet hours keeps its recipient and text (cleared when sent or closed) and is sent by `drainHeld` (`src/lib/messaging/service.ts`), called at the end of `POST /api/internal/reminders/run` (`src/lib/messaging/drain.ts`). It passes the policy, the kill switches and the breaker again.
- **Webhook secret**: on connect, the app sets the Evolution webhook with `jwt_key` = `EVOLUTION_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, so Evolution signs each call (`Authorization: Bearer <HS256 JWT>`). **The n8n side is not in place** (read-only check of 2026-11-20): neither workflow has a webhook guard or JWT Auth. Rollout steps are in `n8n/docs/cambios-n8n-2026-11-20.md` (variable in each Vercel project, re-set every existing instance's webhook, then turn on JWT Auth).
- **Platform line**: the platform sender (password codes) checks the global kill switch (`platform_flags.globalKillSwitch`). It has no outbox row, because it has no tenant.
- **Not done yet**: the bot replies still leave through the Evolution call of n8n until the workflow switches its send node to `POST /api/internal/whatsapp/send` (needs approval and an import); the per-instance key migration for existing instances (D1) needs a check of Evolution's token API before a script is written; the held-message drain has not run against a real instance yet. See also "Pending after QA 2026-11-20".
- **Cancelling from the panel (QA 2026-11-20)**: `PATCH /api/appointments/[id]` writes the status first, then removes the mirror event (`removeMirrorEvent`, `src/lib/appointments-service.ts`) and sends the patient's cancellation notice at the same time (`Promise.all`); they used to run one after the other (calendar up to 8 s, WhatsApp up to 15 s) and Cancel looked frozen. The pressed agenda button shows a spinner and "Cancelando…" (`busyStatus` on `AppointmentCard`). Wiring tests: `tests/qa-2026-11-20-wiring.test.ts`.

### WhatsApp line number and message ids (QA 2026-11-20, second round)

- **"Conectado:" with no number**: the line's number (`connected_apps.waPhoneNumber`) used to be saved only by `GET /api/apps/whatsapp/status`, which runs only while the `/admin/apps` modal waits for the QR scan, and Evolution fills `ownerJid` a moment after the connection opens; a line could stay connected with no number for good (newy did). Now `GET /api/apps/whatsapp/config` (the WhatsApp screen) asks Evolution once when the line is connected and has no number, and saves it (`needsPhoneBackfill`, `phoneFromJid` in `src/lib/whatsapp-phone.ts`); `POST /api/internal/whatsapp/connection` saves the `wuid` of an open event; the adapter also reads the nested `instance.ownerJid` shape and never falls back to another instance of the list. With no number the header says "Conectado" without the colon. Tests: `tests/whatsapp-phone.test.ts`.
- **Connection events do not reach the app**: the chat workflow has no node that calls `/api/internal/whatsapp/connection` (read-only check of 2026-11-20), so `lastConnectedAt`, the restriction pause on a 401/403 close and the `wuid` number are never written from events today.
- **Message ids unique per business**: WhatsApp gives a message the same id on both sides of a chat. When both numbers are on the platform (two businesses writing to each other, or a test phone that is itself an instance), the first business that stored it took it from the other one, because `whatsapp_messages.keyId` was unique in the whole database: the sidebar showed the last message but the chat did not. The key is now unique per business (`@@unique([tenantId, keyId])`); sync and the panel send upsert on `tenantId_keyId`. Two steps: `prisma/sql/34-message-key-per-tenant` (adds the per-business index next to the global one) is **applied to production** (2026-11-20, after a verified backup and an approval, tested on a throwaway copy); `prisma/sql/35-message-key-drop-global` (drops the global index) **is applied to production** (read-only check on 2026-11-20: the global `keyId` index is gone). It had to come after the code that upserts on `tenantId_keyId` (older code used `ON CONFLICT ("keyId")`); whether it ran after that code was deployed was not verified. Until 35 ran, sync logs and skips a message another business already stored (P2002) instead of failing. Tests: `tests/message-key-per-tenant.test.ts`.
- **Sync trusts the instance name**: `whatsapp/sync` takes the business id from the instance name and does not check that the instance is connected in the app, so an instance removed from the app (doctorgcenter, elfar on 2026-11-20) still stores messages. Not changed yet.


## WhatsApp Connection and Evolution API Integration

- **One Evolution client (D1, 2026-11-18)**: every call to Evolution goes through `src/lib/messaging/evolution-adapter.ts`. It validates TLS (no `rejectUnauthorized: false` anywhere), times out, uses each instance's own token, and never returns or logs the provider response body (it can echo a number or the message text). `whatsapp.ts`, `platform-whatsapp.ts`, `whatsapp-qr.ts`, `connect/whatsapp` and `whatsapp/status` all use it. Never add a raw `fetch` to Evolution; extend the adapter.
- **Keys**: `EVOLUTION_API_KEY` is the global key, used **server-side only** for `/instance/create` and `/instance/fetchInstances`, and is **never stored in the database**. Each instance has its own token (Evolution 2.3.7 returns it as `hash` on create); it is stored in `connected_apps.accessToken` and used for sends, QR, status and `webhook/set`. `instanceKeyOf(stored, global)` falls back to the global key for legacy rows, and `whatsapp/status` upgrades such a row to the real instance token when it next reads it. Backfill: `local-utils/whatsapp-instance-tokens.ts`.
- **Signed webhooks**: the connection signs every webhook call with a short-lived JWT built from `EVOLUTION_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (the `jwt_key` header → `Authorization: Bearer <HS256, 10-min expiry>`). The workflow does NOT verify that signature today (read in the live workflow on 2026-11-27): a forged `messages.upsert` POST would be accepted. Closing it is a separate ticket. Keep the same secret in `.env`, in the hosting and, once verification is on, in the workflow's credential.
- **Required flags when creating an instance**:
  When creating or connecting an instance in `POST /api/apps/connect/whatsapp`, the following flags are strictly applied in the `/instance/create` payload as well as in `/settings/set` and `/webhook/set`:
  1. `events: ["MESSAGES_UPSERT"]` (fires the webhook on incoming messages).
  2. `base64` of the webhook: **false for a business in `WA_MEDIA_ENABLED_TENANTS`, true for the rest** (#154, branch `feat/182-media-origin`). `webhookConfig` always takes it explicitly. See "Webhook by the list" below.
  3. `rejectCall: true` (automatically rejects voice calls).
  4. `groupsIgnore: true` (ignores group messages).
  5. `readMessages: true` (marks received messages as read).
  6. `alwaysOnline: true` (keeps the session always active on WhatsApp).
  7. `readStatus: false` (disables reading WhatsApp statuses).
  8. `syncFullHistory: false`.
- **Reliable connection detection (`/api/apps/whatsapp/status`)**:
  - Queries `fetchInstances?instanceName=${instanceName}` on Evolution API.
  - Checks `connectionStatus === "open"` and extracts the phone number from `ownerJid` immediately.
  - **Self-renewing QR**: while the owner waits, `GET /api/apps/whatsapp/status` requests a new code from Evolution (`/instance/connect/<instance>`) when the stored one is more than 25 seconds old (`QR_TTL_MS`). After 8 renewals (`MAX_QR_REFRESHES`, about 3 minutes) the status is `qr_expired` and the modal shows "El código QR expiró" (The QR code expired) with "Generar nuevo código" (Generate new code), which calls `POST /api/apps/connect/whatsapp` again. The issue time and the count live in `ConnectedApp.metadata` (`qrIssuedAt`, `qrRefreshes`). Helpers in `src/lib/whatsapp-qr.ts`. See spec 19.3.
- **Disconnect is two steps (2026-11-24)**: the screen calls `disconnectApp` (server action, and `DELETE /api/apps/[provider]` for other clients). Step 1, `startWhatsAppDisconnect` (`src/lib/whatsapp-disconnect-service.ts`), sends the logout with the instance token and returns at once; it marks the row `waStatus = disconnecting`. If Evolution gives no answer at all, the disconnect does not start (`evolution_unavailable`). Step 2, `checkWhatsAppDisconnectAction` (every 3 seconds, up to 2 minutes, from the click only), calls `removeIfClosed` (`src/lib/whatsapp-disconnect.ts`): while the session is open it waits; once `close`, or when Evolution no longer has the instance, it deletes the instance with the global key and then the row. A failed delete keeps the row, marked `disconnect_failed`. Nothing resumes by itself: leaving the screen during the wait does not stop the loop started by the click, and reopening the screen does not restart it (`whatsapp-disconnect-state.ts` holds the two marks). Evolution closes the session a few seconds (sometimes minutes) after the logout answer, which is why step 2 waits. Tests: `tests/whatsapp-disconnect.test.ts`, `tests/evolution-adapter.test.ts`.
- **Owner number on connect (2026-11-24)**: `GET /api/apps/whatsapp/status` reads the owner number from Evolution (`ownerJid`); when the instance is open but the number is not there yet, it reads again up to 3 times, one second apart (`readOwnerPhone` in `src/lib/whatsapp-phone.ts`). The connect modal (`src/app/admin/apps/WhatsAppConnectModal.tsx`) shows the number the answer carries and closes after 1.2 seconds to `/admin/whatsapp`; if the number is still missing it shows the line without one (the WhatsApp screen backfills it, see `needsPhoneBackfill`). Tests: `tests/whatsapp-owner-phone.test.ts`.
- **No handshake interruption**: The recurring call to `/instance/connect` during polling, which restarted the socket while the phone was scanning the QR code, was removed.
- **Immediate modal close (`WhatsAppModal`)**:
  - Polling every 2 seconds in `/admin/apps`. On detecting `status === "connected"`, it shows the visual success confirmation and closes the modal automatically after 1.2 seconds.
  - When the modal opens, it performs an immediate check to close itself if the session was already linked.

## WhatsApp files in the panel (ticket #182)

**State on 2026-11-27:** the base (PR #188, `dd819c3`) is in production since 07:47 UTC and `prisma/sql/36-whatsapp-media` is applied to the real database, but the switch is off for every business. The production origin, the fetch at arrival, the round tied to the run's clock and the webhook by the list are in branch `feat/182-media-origin` (not merged). Pending work: `docs/STATUS.md`. Evidence of the local test: `docs/qa/182-archivos-panel-whatsapp.md`.

- **What it is**: a photo, audio, video (also the round video note, `ptvMessage`), document or sticker of a chat message, from the patient or sent by the business from its phone, gets one row in `whatsapp_media`. The copy lives 30 days in a private store and the panel shows it through a signed link that expires in 5 minutes. n8n never receives or passes the file, and the base64 an event may carry is never stored.
- **Rules** (pure, `src/lib/wa-media-rules.ts`, tests `tests/wa-media-rules.test.ts`; owner decisions of 2026-11-24 to 2026-11-26):
  - Size: `MAX_MEDIA_BYTES` 16 MB per file, checked BEFORE the download from the size the message declares. A message that does not say its size is never downloaded (`SIZE_UNKNOWN`).
  - Per contact: `MAX_MEDIA_PER_CONTACT_PER_DAY` 30 files received per business day (`Tenant.timeZone`, `contactDayWindow`). The contact is the chat. Only files let in count (`CONTACT_COUNTED_STATUSES`); files the business sends never count.
  - Per business and platform: `MAX_TENANT_MEDIA_BYTES` 1 GB held per business, and 1 GB for the whole platform (`WA_MEDIA_PLATFORM_MAX_BYTES`, `platformMaxBytesOf`; a missing or invalid value is 1 GB, never "no limit"). Hidden files count. Checked in this order: view once, unknown size, too large, contact, business, platform (`admitMedia`).
  - 30 days: `destroyAfter` is `receivedAt` + 30 days and nothing moves it (`destroyAfterOf`). After destruction the row stays without the person's data: caption, file name and path are cleared (`destroyedPatch`).
  - Soft delete: owner and staff (`whatsapp.chats`) hide and restore a stored copy; `whatsapp_media_trail` keeps who, with which role and when. Hiding never moves `destroyAfter`. A link asked before hiding can still open until it expires (at most 5 minutes).
  - View once: never downloaded. Recognised from the outer keys (`viewOnceMessage`, `viewOnceMessageV2`, `viewOnceMessageV2Extension`, also when the wrapper comes empty) or from `viewOnce: true` inside the media (`unwrapWaMessage`, `src/lib/wa-message-content.ts`).
  - What the panel draws: the type is read from the first bytes (`sniffMime`), never taken from the sender. Only a short list is shown in the chat (JPEG, PNG, WebP; OGG, MP3, M4A, AAC; MP4). Anything that arrives as a document only downloads with its name; a PDF opens in another tab (`displayModeOf`).
- **States** (`WhatsAppMediaStatus`): `RECEIVED` (noted by sync, not decided yet), `PENDING` (let in, to download), `STORED`, `TOO_LARGE`, `SIZE_UNKNOWN`, `CONTACT_LIMIT`, `TENANT_LIMIT`, `PLATFORM_LIMIT`, `VIEW_ONCE`, `FAILED` (after `MAX_MEDIA_ATTEMPTS` = 5), `DESTROYED`.
  - How the last attempt closes a row (`closingStatus`, `src/lib/wa-media-ingest.ts`): a `PENDING` row that never arrived ends `FAILED` with the code of the last failure (`download_http`, `download_timeout`…). A `RECEIVED` row whose message the connection no longer has (`message_not_found` five times, for example after a line was reconnected and its instance re-created) also ends `FAILED`, with `failureReason` `message_not_found`. Only a row that could never be read at all (timeouts, errors) ends `SIZE_UNKNOWN`. A message that exists but declares no size is closed `SIZE_UNKNOWN` by admission, without a download. Reason (arch, 2026-11-27): `SIZE_UNKNOWN` means "the message did not tell its size" and is counted; a message that is gone must not blur that count or show the assistant a false notice.
- **Path of a file**: `whatsapp/sync` stores the message line as before and, for a business that is on, notes the file (`noteMediaFromSync`, `src/lib/wa-media-data.ts`: one row per `tenantId` + `keyId`; view once is closed here). `ingestMedia` (`src/lib/wa-media-ingest.ts`, everything outside injected) takes a row from `RECEIVED` to stored: the origin describes the FULL message by its key (the workflow's trimmed shape is never enough: it carries no size, no caption and no view-once mark), `admitMediaRow` counts and writes the verdict in one transaction behind an advisory lock (two arrivals at once never pass a limit), then the bytes are downloaded, the real size is checked again and the store keeps them under `<tenantId>/<mediaId>` (the same path on every try, so a copy left by a cut attempt is overwritten, never duplicated). Without an origin or a store, or for a business that is off, nothing is attempted and no attempt is used (`no_origin`, `no_store`, `disabled`).
- **Origin** (`src/lib/wa-media-origin.ts`): the line is always the one of the row's own business, read from its own `connected_apps` row (instance and key; only when `waStatus` is `connected`), never from a request. Two reads in the adapter: `findMessageByKey` (`POST /chat/findMessages` with a page of 10: in 2.3.7 `offset` is the page size, newest first, read in its source on 2026-11-27; the record of that exact id and side gives size, type, name, caption and view once before any download; any other record is "not found") and `mediaByKey` (`POST /chat/getBase64FromMediaMessage` with the key alone: id, chat, side; an answer without bytes is an error; an answer that declares more than `MAX_MEDIA_RESPONSE_BYTES`, the base64 of 16 MB plus 64 KB, is refused without reading it and the row ends `TOO_LARGE`). Errors are short codes: `timeout`, `network`, `http`, `no_line`, `too_large`. Timeouts: `DESCRIBE_TIMEOUT_MS` 3 s, `DOWNLOAD_TIMEOUT_MS` 12 s, and every upload `STORE_TIMEOUT_MS` 5 s.
- **Fetch at arrival** (`fetchMediaAtArrival`, `src/lib/wa-media-data.ts`): right after sync notes the row, a file that declares at most `INLINE_MAX_BYTES` (3 MiB: the measured 2.1 MB video took 871 ms, about 2.5 MB/s) is fetched inside ONE deadline, `INLINE_DEADLINE_MS` 6 s, for the whole attempt (describe, download, store, row). The workflow waits 10 s for sync (9 nodes, read on 2026-11-27; "Store Owner Message" has no error branch), and those calls take 118 to 321 ms. sync answers by the deadline even if a write ignores the signal. A heavier file is let in and left for the round (`deferred`), and an attempt cut by the deadline (`cut`) uses no attempt either. It runs LAST in sync, after every write of the chat's state (the bot pause when the owner writes, the end of a help request, the loop guard), so a slow download or a cut request never puts them at risk. A failure never breaks the sync.
- **Store** (`src/lib/wa-media-storage.ts`, the only door): S3 protocol with a key limited to the store (never the database key), official client pinned to an exact version. Three operations: put, signed read link of `SIGNED_URL_SECONDS` (300), remove. Paths are only `<uuid>/<uuid>`. The download name and the type the browser sees go in the signed link, set by the server when it signs (`contentDispositionFor`, `safeDownloadName`): the provider stores a `Content-Disposition` given at upload but does not serve it back (checked on 2026-11-25), while the link overrides work (checked on 2026-11-26). Errors are a short code; the provider's message, the endpoint and the keys never leave the module.
- **Panel routes** (all `requirePermission("whatsapp.chats")`, business from the session): `GET /api/apps/whatsapp/messages` adds `media` (a summary, never a link; `src/lib/wa-media-summary.ts`); `GET /api/apps/whatsapp/media/[id]` returns the signed link (`decideMediaLink`, `src/lib/wa-media-access.ts`: the stored path must equal `<session business>/<id>`; another business's id and an invented one both answer 404; hidden, expired or not stored answers 410; never cached); `POST .../media/[id]/hide` and `/restore`. Deleting a chat (`DELETE /api/apps/whatsapp/chats/[id]`) marks its files for destruction and clears their caption and name in the same transaction.
- **Clean-up and retries**: `runMediaPurge` (`src/lib/wa-media-purge.ts`) runs at the end of `POST /api/internal/reminders/run`, in its own error block: it destroys what is due (the 30th day, hidden or not; a deleted chat) for every business, and marks a row destroyed only after the store removed the copy. Then it retries the files still to fetch, only for the businesses on the list (`listRetryable` filters by `tenantId`; an empty list asks nothing). The rows of a business taken off the list stay as they are, use no attempt, and are destroyed on their day 30; they resume if it is switched on again within those days (no new status: the statuses are a database enum).
- **The round's clock**: the workflow node "Run Reminders" waits 60 s (read on 2026-11-27; runs with sends took 0.8 to 9.7 s, the slowest run ever 18.2 s). The clock starts when the request comes in (`runClockFrom`). A row to destroy starts only when `DESTROY_SLOT_MS` (the store's 5 s removal timeout) still fits, and a file to retry only when its whole slot (`FILE_SLOT_MS` 20 s = 3 + 12 + 5) still ends before `RUN_DEADLINE_MS` 45 s (`fitsInRun`). What is not started is not touched and uses no attempt (`destroyLeftForNextRun`, `leftForNextRun`); a file started that fails uses one. A file is retried once it has been idle `RETRY_IDLE_MS` 60 s (`retryCutoff`): the attempt at arrival ends within 6 s and the rounds never overlap, so a file deferred at arrival enters the next round (0 to 15 minutes).
- **Webhook by the list** (`src/lib/wa-webhook-rev.ts`, #154): the webhook stops carrying the file only for the businesses on `WA_MEDIA_ENABLED_TENANTS`. The 15-minute round (before the file retries, same clock) applies it by itself, one call per line and only when the mark `connected_apps.metadata.webhookBase64` differs from what the list wants (`decideWebhook`). No mark means the webhook the line was created with (base64 true), so a business that is off is never called; a business taken off the list gets base64 true back. The address is the one stored on the line (`waWebhookUrl`). Without that address, the connection variables or `EVOLUTION_WEBHOOK_SECRET` the line is skipped with a code and nothing is sent: `/webhook/set` replaces the whole config, so sending it without headers would drop the signature. The mark is written only after the connection accepted (`WEBHOOK_SET_TIMEOUT_MS` 5 s); a failure retries next round. The line's state is read before and after (`STATE_TIMEOUT_MS` 2 s): a line that is not "open" right before is skipped (`line_not_connected`), and a line not "open" right after is counted in `notOpenAfter` of the job's answer (`media.webhooks`) and logged as `[wa-webhook] webhook change`. That count is the measurement that a webhook change does not cut an open line (not measured yet). `connect/whatsapp` uses the same list for a new or reconnected line and writes the mark it applied. The status route can rewrite `metadata` at the same moment; losing the mark costs one repeated, idempotent call. Do not use "Desconectar" to change the webhook: it deletes the instance and the whole `connected_apps` row, with `waContext` and the risk state.
- **Screen**: `src/components/admin/whatsapp/media/` (`MediaBubble`, `MediaImage`, `MediaDocument`, `MediaNotice`, `useMediaUrl`, `useAppliedSrc`, pure rules in `media-view.ts`, tests `tests/wa-media-view.test.ts`). A renewed link never interrupts an audio or video that is playing. With a file, its caption shows and the marker (`[Imagen]`) never does (`bubbleTextOf`, `MEDIA_MARKERS`).
- **Bot**: `bot/media` adds one line to its reply only for a business that is on: files may be kept up to 30 days, with the link to the privacy notice (`customerMessages.mediaStoredNotice`). `classifyInbound` now treats view once and the round video note as files; the workflow's file guard does not let them through yet, so this takes effect when its delivery is pasted.
- **Switch**: `WA_MEDIA_ENABLED_TENANTS` lists the business ids that are on (comma separated); empty or missing is off for everyone. Off: sync notes nothing, `messages` returns `media: null` without reading the tables, the three media routes answer 404, nothing is fetched or retried, and the line keeps the usual webhook. Deleting a chat and the destruction do not look at the switch, so a business that was switched off can still destroy what it stored.
- **Order to ship**: database first. `messages` (for a business that is on), chat delete and the clean-up read the new tables, so `up.sql` of 36 goes to the real database before the merge. The two tables enable row level security in `up.sql`, like every other table (see `schema-changes.md`).
- **Measured on 2026-11-26** with real files sent to the test business (photo 45 KB, voice note 8 KB, photo with caption 26 KB, video 2.1 MB), read-only: the WhatsApp connection finds a message by its key with its size, type, caption and view-once mark before any download, returns the bytes with the key alone, and lists new messages by date. The file name the download returns is invented when the message has none: the name comes from the message, never from the download. Not measured: availability after one hour or one day, documents, voice notes recorded on iOS (a known issue of this connection version), videos of 10 to 16 MB.
